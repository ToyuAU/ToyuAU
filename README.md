## 👨‍💻 What I'm working on

Right now, most of my time goes into **Deployed AI** and building managed AI systems for businesses.

A lot of that involves:

- 🦞 Deploying and extending **OpenClaw**
- 🤖 Building autonomous AI agents and workflows
- 🔌 Connecting AI to Gmail, Microsoft 365, CRMs, ERPs and internal systems
- 🖥️ Building reliable local AI infrastructure on Linux
- 🧠 Working with local models, embeddings, transcription and knowledge systems
- ⚙️ Automating repetitive business operations
- 🛠️ Building custom software and integrations when APIs aren't enough
- 🔐 Designing systems around permissions, security and controlled access

I'm particularly interested in moving AI beyond chat and into systems that can actually **use tools, access company knowledge and complete work**.

- **Infrastructure:** Ubuntu, macOS, Cloudflare, Tailscale
